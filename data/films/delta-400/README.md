# ILFORD DELTA 400

The film the model's development kinetics were fitted to: a silver negative whose
sheet prints a contrast-against-time chart for four developers, one characteristic
curve, and nothing else — no MTF, no granularity, no sensitivity chart, all three
declared rather than invented.

- **The report:** [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) — the curve the kinetics are checked
  against, three declared absences, the speed-point anchor, and what a flat
  panchromatic response means in a three-channel format.
- **The film:** `delta-400.kalofilm` (5,635 bytes) — load it with
  `kalo render --film-file`.
- **The points:** `curves/` — 367 points, the same record in all three files.
- **The figures:** [01-curves](figures/01-curves.png), [02-residuals](figures/02-residuals.png), [03-spectra](figures/03-spectra.png), drawn
  from `curves/` and the file's own tables.

Headline numbers: the curve climbs to 2.03 above its base; base 0.280; γ 0.694
per decade; residual 0.0094 over 367 points; no MTF, no grain figure, a flat
layer response. At 18 per cent grey the negative sits 0.88 of a density above
its base, and its speed point lands on the ISO rating by construction — the
anchor is the speed point itself, as it is for HP5 PLUS.
