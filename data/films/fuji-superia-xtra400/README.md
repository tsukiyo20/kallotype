# FUJICOLOR SUPERIA X-TRA 400

The consumer 400 that Fuji printed as a bitmap, and the first stock in the
catalogue whose curves were read out of a raster rather than out of vector
paths.

- **The report:** [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) — the bitmap panels, the anchor the
  sheet does not print, and why all three shoulders sit outside the frame.
- **The film:** `fuji-superia-xtra400.kalofilm` (6,147 bytes) — load it with
  `kalo render --film-file`.
- **The points:** `curves/` — 128 points per layer.
- **The figures:** [01-curves](figures/01-curves.png),
  [02-residuals](figures/02-residuals.png), [03-spectra](figures/03-spectra.png).

Headline numbers: γ 0.624 / 0.672 / 0.698 per decade; base 0.127 / 0.459 / 0.745;
residuals 0.0047 / 0.0049 / 0.0061; σ 3.40 µm from the sheet's own MTF; no
reference exposure printed, so the anchor is the C-41 printing aim at +1.0291.
