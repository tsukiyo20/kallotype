# KODAK PROFESSIONAL T-MAX 100

The slow panchromatic film, and the second silver stock whose blur comes from
its own MTF.

- **The report:** [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) — the three development curves, the
  7.5-minute choice, the Gaussian fit and the two declared absences.
- **The film:** `kodak-tmax-100.kalofilm` (5,624 bytes) — load it with
  `kalo render --film-file`.
- **The points:** `curves/` — 128 points, the same record in all three files.
- **The figures:** [01-curves](figures/01-curves.png),
  [02-residuals](figures/02-residuals.png), [03-spectra](figures/03-spectra.png).

Headline numbers: γ 0.696 per decade; base 0.218; rms 0.0153 over 128 points;
blur σ 1.42 µm. Data and prose: CC BY-SA 4.0. See [../LICENSE](../LICENSE) and
[../NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).
