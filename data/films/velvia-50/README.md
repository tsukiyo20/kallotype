# FUJICHROME VELVIA 50 (RVP)

An E-6 colour reversal whose curves, three dyes, layer sensitivities, MTF and
granularity are all from its own sheet.

- **The report:** [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) — the order rule that reads three
  overprinted curves, the shared tail and what it costs, the fits, the anchor,
  and the four panels on AF3-960E page 7.
- **The film:** `velvia-50.kalofilm` (6,026 bytes) — load it with
  `kalo render --film-file`.
- **The points:** `curves/` — 672, 655 and 591 digitised points.
- **The figures:** [01-curves](figures/01-curves.png), [02-residuals](figures/02-residuals.png), [03-spectra](figures/03-spectra.png), drawn
  from `curves/` and the file's own tables.

Headline numbers: unexposed slide 3.32 (red), 3.77 (green), 3.68 (blue); clear
film 0.11–0.12; γ 2.39, 2.65 and 2.47 per decade; residuals 0.023–0.027;
sensitivities read at 451, 549 and 651 nm; dyes at 445, 540 and 660 nm;
σ 4.28 µm; grain 2.26 grains/µm² inverted from the sheet's published rms
granularity of 9, at the density that granularity was measured at.
