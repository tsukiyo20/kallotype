# FUJIFILM 200

A consumer sheet with two charts and no more; three of the five modelled fields are
declared borrowings, and the report says why that is still a film.

- **The report:** [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) — the sheet, the fit, the anchor and what
  is borrowed rather than measured.
- **The film:** `fuji-200.kalofilm` (5,522 bytes) — load it with
  `kalo render --film-file`.
- **The points:** `curves/` — 128 points per layer.
- **The figures:** [01-curves](figures/01-curves.png), [02-residuals](figures/02-residuals.png), [03-spectra](figures/03-spectra.png).

Headline numbers: γ 0.531 / 0.615 / 0.665 per decade; measured fields: curves, layer sensitivities.
