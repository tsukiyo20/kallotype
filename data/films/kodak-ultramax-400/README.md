# KODAK ULTRA MAX 400

The consumer colour negative that prints its own reference exposure, and the
film whose three curves fit tighter than any other Kodak sheet in the series.

- **The report:** [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) — the sheet, the printed anchor, the
  measured mask and the two declared absences.
- **The film:** `kodak-ultramax-400.kalofilm` (6,069 bytes) — load it with
  `kalo render --film-file`.
- **The points:** `curves/` — 128 points per layer.
- **The figures:** [01-curves](figures/01-curves.png),
  [02-residuals](figures/02-residuals.png), [03-spectra](figures/03-spectra.png).

Headline numbers: γ 0.510 / 0.533 / 0.597 per decade; base 0.285 / 0.696 / 0.982;
residuals 0.0027 / 0.0046 / 0.0036; `Log H Ref −1.44` printed on the sheet.
